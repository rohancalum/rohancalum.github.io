Speed & scale, faster

September 20, 2026

The Speed & Scale framework and opportunity areas is a great resource for learning how to decarbonize civilization Credit: NASA’s Scientific Visualization Studio. . Moving fast requires clarity, which their taxonomy of technologies initiates. Applying the “Objectives and Key Results (OKRs)” framework, they define outcomes that require innovation within each area.

I wanted to answer:

  1. Which outcomes are highest impact?
  2. Which outcomes are most neglected?
  3. What are the bottlenecks, and can AI solve them?

Interfaces determine how you think, so I used Astra and Codex to reorganize and extend their website to be outcome-first. You can now sort by outcome, see which are most neglected, search by keyword, and rank by quantities.

I didn't review the model's research, so there could be inaccuracies and it’s certainly incomplete (I gave it $100 budget). However, even with limited rigour, you learn a few interesting things:

  • The top 3 highest impact outcomes are: "Always-on clean power production", "drive down cost and reduce land use for intermittent renewables", and "expanding cost-competitive clean heat to higher temperatures"
  • There are 2,306 companies with $80.4B in funding across all outcomes
  • 116/122 outcomes (95%) have more than 10 companies working on them

Future extensions:

  1. Use Codex to research subproblems, companies and people relevant to each outcome
  2. Use Codex to determine where LLM-based agents can solve bottlenecks
  3. Use Codex to build personalized news for aspiring entrepreneurs

I hope this interface helps aspiring founders think in terms of which outcomes they are best positioned to drive impact. In a future post, I'll open-source the code for the website, agent-driven research methodology, and enrich each outcome with the findings. Please get in touch if you’re interested in this!
